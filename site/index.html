What this is — and isn't

  • Vendor-readiness packaging: a complete, evidence-referenced answer set you can defend.
  • Drafted from your real documentation, with every gap flagged and dated, not papered over.
  • Reviewed and approved by you before it reaches your buyer.
This isn't
  • A certification, audit, or legal compliance determination — that call is yours, with counsel if needed.

FAQ

What formats do you handle?

SIG Lite, SIG Full, CAIQ, VSA, and custom buyer spreadsheets — PDF, Excel, or portal exports. If it's a format you haven't seen before, it still gets quoted before anything starts.

When does the 72-hour clock start?

After intake is complete — the questionnaire file plus whatever evidence you're sending is in hand. Waiting on evidence from your side pauses the clock, it doesn't eat into it.

What if my questionnaire is over 150 questions?

You get a quote before any work starts — never a surprise invoice after the fact.

Do you guarantee I'll pass the review?

No — nobody honestly can. What's guaranteed is a complete, evidence-backed, internally consistent answer set that gives your reviewer something real to work with, not a stall.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere but this engagement. Your questionnaire and evidence are never stored in a public repository or run through a consumer AI tool.

What if I need a correction after delivery?

One correction round is included. Anything beyond that is a quick turnaround at the hourly rate, not a new engagement.
