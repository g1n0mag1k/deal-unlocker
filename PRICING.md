# Deal Unlocker — Pricing

Sole-proprietor service under Sui-Generis (Andrew Rogers), Knoxville, TN.
Prices below are list price; founding-client terms are called out where they
apply. Every tier and add-on still runs through the same engagement
guardrail: the client approves every questionnaire answer before it goes
back to their buyer.

## Core engagement

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **One-off questionnaire** | $900 (first 5 clients, "founding" rate) → **$1,500 standard** | Single SIG Lite / CAIQ / custom questionnaire (up to ~150 questions), answer set, Gap Register, Reviewer Brief, 72-hour turnaround. |
| **Rush (24-hour)** | **+40% over the applicable base fee** (e.g. $2,100 at standard rate) | Same deliverable, expedited. Reflects real after-hours/displaced-work cost of compressing the turnaround, not just a speed tax. |
| **Additional questionnaire** (existing client, no retainer) | **$250–350** | Reuses the client's answer vault; priced below a fresh engagement because the library is already built. |

## Recurring

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Vault Retainer** | **$450–650/mo** (~$5.4K–7.8K/yr) | Unlimited questionnaires during the term, answer vault kept current, Gap Register updated as remediation items close. Replaces the old flat $99/mo maintenance line — this is the primary retention and recurring-revenue lever. |
| **Annual Volume Contract** | **$8,000–15,000/yr** | For founders closing 4+ enterprise deals/year. Bundles Vault Retainer + quarterly posture review + priority (48-hr) turnaround into one contract. |

## Add-ons (stack on top of any tier)

| Add-on | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick-Validate** | **$150–400** | Client (or another tool) already has draft answers; this is review/correction only, no full drafting. Low-friction entry product — upsell path into the full engagement. |
| **Reviewer Defense Call** | **$300–600** | A paid 1–2 hr session joining the buyer's security-reviewer follow-up call to defend the answer set directly. |
| **BAA / Subprocessor Contract Redline** | **$750–1,500** | Separate SOW. Negotiates the actual contract exhibit language once the questionnaire itself clears — the next real deal-blocker after the SIG is answered. |
| **Fundraising / M&A Security Diligence Pack** | **$5,000–10,000** | Timed to a funding round or acquisition, when an investor/acquirer runs their own diligence pass and budget exists for it. |
| **HITRUST e1 Readiness Roadmap** | **$5,000–15,000** | Positions the client ahead of the hospital-system shift toward shared HITRUST credentials instead of a new bespoke SIG per buyer. Highest-ticket, most differentiated line. |

## Why these numbers

Benchmarked against direct competitors (Polaris Cybersecurity $750–1,500,
DealProof Desk $349/$699, Forward Security Labs $250/$750+, Xorabyte
$1,500) and adjacent comps: SecurityPal's observed annual contract values
(~$11.8K–$94.9K, avg ~$33K) and Whistic's (~$12.7K–$43.6K, avg ~$20.6K) set
the ceiling the Annual Volume Contract is priced well under, since this is a
solo practice, not a platform. The 25–50% regulated-vertical premium on
vCISO retainers supports pricing HITRUST and the diligence pack above
generic rates. See the retainer/rush-fee research discussed with Claude
(2026-10-29) for full sourcing.

## Guardrails that apply to every tier

- Never say "compliant" or "in violation" — that's a legal conclusion.
- Client approves every answer before submission.
- Client data never enters a public repo or consumer AI tier.
- E&O insurance in place before the third client.
